### Method and provenance

The fit forms its target contrast from the final unpadded token, applies it at valid target positions, averages source-position gradients per prompt, averages classes independently, subtracts the class means, and normalizes each source layer. Valid positions exclude `skip_first` and the final real token.

The method is motivated by Anthropic's J-lens work. VJP-delta implementation provenance is `wassname/vjp-steering@cb03382ebd0cc9cad615d169f42e68e8ae3e12a7`. This contribution is independently implemented from the documented mathematics and destination contracts. It does not copy or assert a license for the provenance repository.
